Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you use our website, fill out forms, or communicate with our team. This may include your name, email address, company name, phone number, and WhatsApp number.

We also collect travel-related information that you share with us through our enquiry forms, such as destination preferences, travel dates, group size, required services, and any special requirements or dietary needs for your clients.

Additionally, we may collect communication data including records of correspondence between you and our team, which helps us provide better service and maintain continuity in our business relationship.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to respond to your enquiries, provide travel ground services, and deliver the solutions you request. This includes preparing proposals, coordinating restaurant reservations, arranging guides, and organizing transfers and transportation.

Your information also helps us communicate with you regarding your ongoing requirements, share relevant updates about our services or destinations, and maintain a professional business relationship with your agency.

We may also use your information to improve our website, services, and overall customer experience, as well as to ensure the security and integrity of our platform.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. Your data is used exclusively for the purpose of providing our B2B DMC services.

We may share your information with trusted service partners, such as restaurant partners, guide networks, and transportation providers, but only to the extent necessary to deliver the ground services you have requested. These partners are expected to handle your information with the same level of care and confidentiality.

We may also disclose your information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and property of DMC SOURCE, our clients, or the public.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. This includes secure storage, access controls, and regular security assessments.

While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to maintaining industry-standard protections.

5. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze website traffic, and understand how visitors interact with our content. Cookies are small data files stored on your device that help us improve the functionality and performance of our website.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain features of the website, but you will still be able to access the core content and functions.

6. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites or third-party services that are not operated by DMC SOURCE. We are not responsible for the content, privacy practices, or policies of any third-party websites.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit through links on our site. Your interactions with external websites are governed by their respective terms and policies.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you. You may request a copy of your data at any time by contacting us.

You also have the right to request corrections to any inaccurate or incomplete personal information, and to request the deletion of your personal information, subject to any legal obligations we may have to retain certain data.

If you wish to exercise any of these rights or have questions about how your information is handled, please contact us at info@dmcsource.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, services, or applicable regulations. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we collect, use, and protect your information. Your continued use of our website and services after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
